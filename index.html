WebSpectrometer2.0

Free, open-source software for DIY and webcam spectrometers that runs entirely in your browser — live, calibrated spectra and light-quality measurements, no installation required.

  • Automatic wavelength calibration from reference lamps, spectral response calibration
  • Light quality: CCT, Duv, CRI Ra, R9 · UV-A share · UVC/UVB/UVA/colour/IR band bar
  • Spectral line identification with 2nd/3rd-order ghost flagging, FWHM, compare, HDR
  • Transmission of glasses and filters, absorbance (Beer–Lambert), CSV/TXT export, PDF report

🔒 Your privacy: all image processing happens locally in your browser — no data is ever uploaded.

Runs in Chromium-based browsers (Chrome, Edge, Opera…); Firefox and Safari are not supported (no Web Serial) · Works on Linux, macOS and Windows · GNU GPL v3. Based on jayden-sudo/WebSpectrometer and Theremino Spectrometer V5.0; developed by radoslawo81 with Claude (Anthropic).
